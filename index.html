How to Negotiate a Dog Breeding Agreement

Documents, Contracts, and Fair Settlements

June 13, 2026

Why agreements matter

  • No big surprises
    You will not suddenly face a large stud fee or be asked to give away a puppy without planning
  • Clear payment rules
    The stud owner cannot change the deal (for example, asking for money instead of the agreed puppy)
  • Defined actions
    If no puppies are born, the contract can say that the next mating is free
  • Exact timing
    The agreement shows when to pay or when to give the puppy
  • Chance to compare
    You have time to look at different studs, check documents, and compare prices and conditions
  • Smart choice
    You can make a careful, informed decision without last‑minute problems

    Pedigree number check
    The dam’s owner must provide the sire’s pedigree number when registering the litter.
    Stud service certificate
    FCI requires a signed certificate from the stud owner confirming the mating took place.
    Payment of stud fee
    According to FCI International Breeding Rules, the stud fee must be paid and acknowledged by the stud owner.

    Transparency and Fairness in Breeding

    • Clear contracts - written agreements show all terms, so no one can change the deal later.
    • Equal treatment - both dam and sire owners have the same rights and duties.
    • Club oversight - kennel clubs check pedigree numbers, stud service certificates, and confirm payment before registering puppies.
    • No hidden costs - fees and conditions are agreed in advance, preventing last‑minute surprises.
    • Health protection - mandatory health checks and hereditary tests keep breeding safe and responsible.
    • Fair choice - breeders can compare studs, documents, and prices openly before making a decision.
